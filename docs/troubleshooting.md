
# Troubleshooting

Use this page to resolve common PulseDesk issues.

## Notifications are not received

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Notifications**.
3. Verify that the required notifications are enabled.
4. Save your changes.

## Unable to sign in

1. Verify your account credentials.
2. Confirm that you can access your support workspace.
3. Contact your administrator if the problem persists.

## Still need help?

Contact your PulseDesk support administrator.
