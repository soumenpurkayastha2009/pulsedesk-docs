
# Getting Started

Follow these steps to begin using PulseDesk.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A PulseDesk account.
- Access to your team's support workspace.

## Sign in to PulseDesk

1. Open the PulseDesk application.
2. Enter your account credentials.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. Verify that your support workspace appears.
